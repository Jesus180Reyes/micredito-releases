# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Core FInanciero App** we are collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Personal Information
The App allows users to voluntarily enter personal information, which may include but is not limited to:
- Names
- Identification numbers
- Contact information
- Financial or credit-related information entered in forms

This information is provided directly by the user and is used solely for the functionality of the App.

### b. Media and Camera Access
The App uses the device **camera** to:
- Capture photos or documents
- Register signatures or visual evidence required by the App’s features

The App does **not** access the camera in the background and only does so when the user explicitly initiates the action.

### c. Local Storage
The App stores information **locally on the device** to:
- Save forms and progress
- Enable offline functionality
- Improve reliability in low or no internet conditions

Data stored locally is not accessed by third parties.

---

## 2. Permissions Used

The App may request the following permissions:

- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**  
  Used only to capture images or documents required for app functionality.

- **Storage Access (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or related permissions)**  
  Used to save and retrieve files generated by the App (such as documents, images, or forms) that must remain accessible even when the app is restarted or used offline.

The App cannot fully rely on more restrictive storage alternatives (such as the Storage Access Framework or Media Store) because it requires controlled access to app-generated files to ensure data integrity, offline availability, and proper file management across sessions.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to:
- Provide and operate the App’s features
- Save and manage user-entered data
- Generate documents or records requested by the user
- Improve app stability and user experience

We do **not** sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

User data is:
- **Not shared** with third parties
- **Not sold or monetized**
- **Not used for advertising or tracking**

All data remains under the control of the user and within the scope of the App’s functionality.

---

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect user information, including:
- Local-only data storage where applicable
- Controlled access to sensitive features
- Limiting permissions to what is strictly necessary

However, no method of storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 6. User Control

Users have full control over the data they enter into the App.  
They may:
- Edit or delete stored information from within the App
- Uninstall the App to remove locally stored data from the device

---

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
